How trading works here

Prices are live. Crypto, the gold token (PAXG) and EUR stream from Binance every second, 24/7. US stocks and ETFs stream trade by trade from Finnhub while Wall Street is open (16:30–23:00 Beirut time, Mon–Fri); add your free key in ⚙ Settings.

Fills are real prices. Crypto market orders fill at Binance's actual best ask (buy) or bid (sell). Stocks fill at the last trade plus a 0.01% spread.

Limit order waits until price reaches your level or better. Tap the chart to set the price. Stop order triggers once price crosses your level, then fills at market.

Sell / Short with no position opens a short: you profit when price falls. Selling while long closes or reduces the long.

Leverage lets a small margin control a bigger position. At 10×, a 9% move against you liquidates the position (90% of the margin lost).

Stop-loss and take-profit close the position automatically. Orders and stops are checked only while the app is open, like a demo account that's switched on.

Fees are 0.10% of the traded value per fill. Your account is saved on this device.
