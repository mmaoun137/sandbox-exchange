# Sandbox Exchange

Paper trading with a $5,000,000 demo balance at live market prices. No real money.

- Crypto, gold (PAXG) and EUR stream every second from Binance's public market data.
- US stocks and ETFs stream from Finnhub during market hours. Add a free key in the app's Settings; it is stored only on your device.

Install on iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
